Summary: These Terms govern all freelance creative engineering, design, digital marketing, video production, and IT consulting services provided by RootByte. By accepting a project estimate, statement of work (SOW), or paying an initiation invoice, you agree to these terms.

01. Engagement & Statements of Work

All client engagements are formally defined through a Statement of Work (SOW), project brief, or written scope confirmation detailing deliverables, milestones, estimated timelines, and commercial terms.

Any scope modifications requested after project kickoff will be evaluated under our Change Order process and may adjust delivery dates and budget accordingly.

02. Intellectual Property & Ownership

RootByte operates under a transparent, client-first intellectual property transfer model:

  • Final Deliverables: Upon receipt of full and final payment for the project, all bespoke source code, visual designs, assets, video edits, and deliverables created specifically for the client become the exclusive property of the client.
  • Studio Pre-existing Assets: RootByte retains ownership of pre-existing proprietary developer tooling, boilerplate frameworks, general libraries, and reusable scripts. The client is granted a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use such components integrated into the deliverables.
  • Portfolio Display: Unless subject to a countersigned Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA), RootByte reserves the right to showcase non-confidential project visuals and case studies in studio portfolios and marketing presentations.

03. Invoicing, Payment & Milestones

Standard studio project billing is structured across milestone tranches:

  • Initiation Deposit: A deposit (typically 30% to 50% depending on scope) is required prior to project scheduling and sprint commencement.
  • Milestone Payments: Remaining balances are tied to verified deliverables (e.g., Phase 02 Prototype Approval, Phase 04 QA Sign-off) and final deployment.
  • Payment Terms: Invoices are payable within 7 business days of issuance via supported direct wire, bank transfer, or verified payment portals.

04. Client Obligations & Feedback Cycles

Timely delivery requires close collaboration. The client agrees to:

  • Provide timely access to necessary brand assets, API credentials, copy, and third-party hosting environments.
  • Review sprint deliverables and provide actionable consolidated feedback within 5 business days of review requests.
  • Designate a single primary point of contact authorized to make project decisions and milestone approvals.

05. Launch Warranty & Support Period

We stand behind our code. Every delivered project includes a complimentary 30-day post-launch warranty period covering:

  • Rectification of software bugs or responsive rendering defects attributable to original code scope.
  • Basic handover guidance and administrative orientation for your internal teams.
  • Assistance with production DNS propagation and cloud server monitoring.

New feature requests, third-party API breaking changes, or client modifications made during the warranty period fall outside warranty and are handled under standard maintenance rates.

06. Limitation of Liability

In no event shall RootByte or its contributors be liable for indirect, incidental, punitive, or consequential damages, including loss of profits, data corruption, or business interruption arising from the use or inability to use the deliverables.

RootByte’s maximum aggregate liability under any contract shall not exceed the total fees actually received by RootByte under the applicable Statement of Work.
