Cookie & Tracking Policy

Cookie Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · Version: 1.1 (Active) · Scope: rootbyte.io
Summary: RootByte values your digital autonomy. We employ only privacy-respecting, minimal cookies and local storage tokens necessary for page responsiveness, interactive scope estimator calculations, and theme persistence. We do not use intrusive third-party cross-site behavioral tracking cookies.

01. Understanding Cookies

Cookies are compact text files stored on your computer or mobile device when accessing modern web applications. They allow websites to remember user actions, retain preferences, and ensure responsive graphics render without lag.

02. Categories of Cookies We Deploy

Our digital studio site utilizes three primary classifications of cookies:

  • Strictly Necessary: Essential for page navigation, security token validation, CSRF attack prevention, and state maintenance when submitting scope inquiries.
  • Functional & Preference: Remember user preferences such as your interactive cost estimation parameters, filter selections, and visual accessibility toggles.
  • Performance & Telemetry: Aggregated, anonymized latency metrics to diagnose WebGL rendering performance, device viewports, and page loading speeds.
Cookie / Storage Key Classification Purpose Retention
rb_theme_state Strictly Necessary Saves user display preference & hardware rendering tier 1 Year
rb_scope_cache Functional Preserves chosen estimator options during active browsing session Session
rb_csrf_token Security Protects contact modal submissions from spoofing attacks Session
rb_perf_telemetry Performance Anonymized asset load times and GPU shader initialization stats 30 Days

03. Third-Party Embedded Technologies

RootByte may reference trusted technical infrastructure partners to deliver high-quality fonts and assets:

  • Google Fonts: Provides low-latency serif and sans-serif typography (Newsreader, Inter, Bodoni Moda). Font requests are served without tracking cookies.
  • Content Delivery Networks (CDNs): Cloudflare and global edge caches to provide fast, geo-distributed delivery of our visual assets and scripts.

04. Managing & Disabling Cookies

You have full control over cookie preferences through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies.
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

Please note that blocking essential cookies may affect the interactive functionality of our Delivery Timeline Estimator and project inquiry modals.
