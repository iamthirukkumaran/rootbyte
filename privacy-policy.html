Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · Version: 1.2 (Active) · Entity: RootByte Digital Studio
Summary: RootByte is committed to protecting your privacy and intellectual property. We only collect the minimal personal and technical information necessary to deliver our engineering, design, marketing, and media services. We never sell, lease, or monetize your data.

01. Scope & Applicability

This Privacy Policy applies to all interactions with RootByte ("we", "us", or "our"), including visits to rootbyte.io, communications through our contact portals, scope inquiry forms, project consultation meetings, and active engineering contracts.

By accessing our services or submitting information through our digital interfaces, you acknowledge and consent to the data practices outlined in this policy.

02. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to scope, initiate, and fulfill creative and technical engineering engagements:

  • Client Contact Details: Full name, professional work email, company name, telephone number, and preferred communication channels submitted via our contact and booking forms.
  • Project Specifications: Technical requirements, project briefs, design assets, repository access, brand collateral, and delivery timelines provided for scoping purposes.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP address, device type, browser specifications, and referral URLs collected automatically through minimal performance telemetry to ensure optimal site rendering and security.

03. How We Utilize Your Information

All data collected is used solely to facilitate professional business deliverables:

  • Generating accurate scope estimations, project timelines, and technical proposals.
  • Fulfilling contracted web development, full-stack architecture, UI/UX design, video editing, and IT services.
  • Sending milestone notifications, invoice summaries, and delivery handovers.
  • Protecting our digital infrastructure against unauthorized access, malicious payloads, and denial-of-service threats.

04. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We maintain strict confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or project data with advertising networks or third-party data brokers. Information is only shared under the following conditions:

  • Infrastructure Providers: Secure cloud infrastructure and email delivery partners (e.g., AWS, Cloudflare, Resend/SendGrid) operating under industry-standard data protection agreements.
  • Client Authorization: Third-party integrations (e.g., GitHub, Figma, Stripe, Vercel) explicitly approved by the client for project deployment.
  • Legal Obligations: Compliance with applicable laws, judicial proceedings, or lawful governmental requests.

05. Data Security & Retention

We enforce robust technical safeguards including end-to-end HTTPS/TLS encryption, restricted least-privilege repository access, and multi-factor authentication across all studio administrative infrastructure.

Client project files and correspondence are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill project warranties, 30-day post-launch support periods, and legal accounting obligations, after which proprietary assets are permanently purged upon request.

06. Your Rights & Data Control

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR, CCPA, and Indian Digital Personal Data Protection standards), you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold concerning your account.
  • Request the rectification of inaccurate contact or business details.
  • Request the permanent deletion of your project correspondence and contact records.
  • Opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

07. Contact & Inquiries
